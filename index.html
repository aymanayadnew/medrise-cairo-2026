MED-RISE: two days in Cairo to bring private capital into Mediterranean water

Long image

A

Answer

The first MED-RISE turned the Rome commitment to fully funded water plans into a two-day track at Cairo Water Week: an open regional conference on 26 October, then an expert day on 27 October focused on real project pipelines.

  1. MED-RISE, Mediterranean Resilience through Investment and Sustainable Ecosystems, was convened by the European Union with the Union for the Mediterranean, GWP-Med and the WES-BCA project.1
  2. Private capital is under 2% of water spending in developing countries, which is the gap MED-RISE targets.2
  3. The results feed the Rome Initiative on Water and the UN 2026 Water Conference in Abu Dhabi, 8 to 10 December.3

Why does private capital matter here?

Private share
Under 2% of water spending in developing countries
Public share
About 91%, from governments and state-owned utilities
Economic risk
Climate-related water scarcity could cost the Middle East and North Africa 6 to 14% of GDP by 2050

The shares come from the World Bank's 2024 review of public water spending.2 The GDP range is a 2018 regional projection.4
